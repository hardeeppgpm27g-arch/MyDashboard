Profile

Turning ground-level product realities into board-ready decisions

Hardeep is an operational-excellence-driven management professional with 34 months of experience, having most recently served as an Assistant Manager at Voltas. A major focus of his career has been product management and inventory management: he successfully planned over ₹6 Crore worth of spare inventory for domestic and overseas clients, using consumption-trend and failure-rate analysis to ensure "near-0" critical stock-outs. This built on his earlier project with Indian Railways, where he developed an inventory-tracking app for store managers to optimize control and prevent stock-outs. At Voltas, he also led OEM coordination — actively engaging with vendors and suppliers on product validation, spare audits, quality assurance, and warranty claims.

His commitment to innovation and excellence was formally recognized in August 2025, when he was honoured with the "High-5" award by the Business Head for developing a product-solutions portal that can be flexibly accessed, cutting the pitch cycle down to minutes from 48 hours. He also had the opportunity to represent the entire product portfolio on a major industry stage at ACREX by ISHRAE, South Asia's premier HVAC expo, held in Mumbai in March 2026.
